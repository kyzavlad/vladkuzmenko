# Performance Complete — Product Brief

Status: validation concept only. No checkout. No nutrition or health claim is final until a contract manufacturer confirms the recipe, specification, labelling and regulatory path.

## Product thesis

Performance Complete is a powdered meal for busy work, training and travel days. It should solve one narrow job: make one reliable meal available in about a minute when cooking is the part of the routine most likely to fail.

The category benchmark is a 90 g / 400 kcal serving with a high plant-protein profile, fibre, fats, carbohydrates and a full micronutrient blend. The final Performance recipe must be independently developed with a qualified manufacturer rather than copied or repacked from any third-party retail product.

## Target profile for supplier RFQ

- 17 servings per pouch
- 90 g target serving
- ~400 kcal target serving
- ~40 g plant protein target
- high-fibre target
- low-sugar target
- complete vitamin/mineral premix target where legally and technically supportable
- plant protein base: pea + rice as starting direction
- flaxseed, tapioca/carbohydrate base, plant-oil powder and optional coconut component as formulation directions
- natural flavour system
- first flavours to quote: Vanilla and Chocolate
- pouch format first, no RTD or bars in v1
- shaker/scoop as optional starter-kit accessories

## Pricing hypothesis

Public validation anchor:
- One-time target: €39.90 / 17 servings (~€2.35 per meal)
- Subscription target: €33.90 / 17 servings (~15% lower, ~€1.99 per meal)

This is a willingness-to-pay test, not an approved selling price. Final pricing is only locked after VAT, manufacturing, packaging, pick/pack, shipping subsidy, payment fees, returns/waste and acquisition economics are known.

Internal launch thresholds to test with suppliers:
- target ex-VAT product + primary packaging COGS: <= €10–12 per pouch
- preferred landed COGS before customer shipping: <= €14
- do not open paid acquisition until contribution margin remains healthy after fulfilment/payment costs
- free shipping should begin at a multi-pouch threshold rather than on a single pouch unless economics prove otherwise

## Validation sequence

1. Publish the concept as an application/waitlist, not a preorder.
2. Collect city/country, routine, current alternative, flavour, frequency and price acceptance.
3. Request RFQs and samples from EU/private-label powder manufacturers.
4. Choose one manufacturing route and obtain formula, nutrition panel, allergen statement, shelf-life, MOQ, unit costs and compliance support.
5. Taste/mixability test with a small group.
6. Lock packaging and claims only after the manufacturer and regulatory review confirm them.
7. Run a small paid pilot.
8. Measure reorder intent, realised gross margin, support load and acquisition economics before expanding SKUs.

## Lean team

No employees required for v1. Use a small contractor/supplier network:
- Founder / product owner: positioning, economics, supplier decision, content and launch
- Contract manufacturer / food technologist: formulation, sourcing, production and QC
- Nutrition / regulatory reviewer: claims, nutrition panel, allergens and market-specific label review
- Packaging designer: pouch and label system after formula is locked
- 3PL / fulfilment partner: only after first paid batch economics are confirmed
- Content/growth: founder-led initially; scale editing/distribution only when a repeatable content angle wins

## Content angles

Problem-first, not supplement-first:
- “The meal that disappears when the day gets busy”
- “Why I stopped trying to meal-prep every perfect day”
- “What I wanted from a 60-second meal before building one”
- “400 calories / 40 g protein is the target. Taste and real convenience still have to earn the launch.”
- behind-the-scenes supplier, flavour and packaging decisions

Primary CTA during validation: Join the pilot / tell us what would make this worth buying.
