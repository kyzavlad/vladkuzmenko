# Owned Ventures Business Models V1

Status: canonical product and website model for the three owned ventures outside client Growth Systems.

Principle: do not present unverified prices, launch dates, availability, results or automation as final. Each venture earns the right to scale through a real end-to-end pilot.

## 1. VisibilityOS

### Positioning
Website intelligence and conversion audit system that shows where a site loses leads, trust and visibility, then turns evidence into a prioritized action plan.

### Customer
Businesses, creators, service providers and platforms that already have a website and need clarity on what to fix first.

### V1 product
URL -> Scan -> Evidence -> Priority -> Human QA -> Action plan -> Delivery -> Re-check.

### Commercial ladder
1. Mini scan / prospect-specific proof: a small number of concrete findings used as a lead magnet or sales proof.
2. Full audit: paid product once delivery time, report quality and willingness-to-pay are validated.
3. Monitoring / re-check: recurring layer only after repeated audits prove demand.
4. Implementation: qualified audit findings can move into Client Growth Systems when the customer wants fixes shipped.

### V1 readiness gate
A real URL can move from request to delivered, human-reviewed audit and follow-up without manual ambiguity.

### Metrics
Request -> completed scan -> delivered audit -> paid audit -> implementation conversation -> implementation.
Also track turnaround time, manual review time, recommendation acceptance and re-check improvement.

### Website promise
Evidence, priorities and a next-step roadmap. Early-access audits remain AI-assisted and personally reviewed until the automated product is proven.

## 2. Warriors Team

### Positioning
Selective private network for people already executing in business, content, skills, training and projects.

### Customer
People who have something real to bring into the circle and want sharper feedback, useful introductions and a higher standard of execution.

### V1 product
Application -> Fit review -> Short conversation -> Accept / Reject -> Onboarding -> Private circle -> Weekly execution rhythm -> Feedback / introductions -> Review.

### Founding-circle operating rhythm
1. Weekly Execution Review: what moved, what is blocked, next concrete move.
2. Problem Rooms: one real problem with enough context for direct useful feedback.
3. Useful Introductions: targeted introductions when two members can materially help each other.

### Monetization
Do not publish a permanent membership price before the founding cohort proves repeated use and willingness to pay. Founding access validates the experience first; paid continuation follows only when value is demonstrated.

### V1 readiness gate
A small founding cohort can be selected, onboarded and run through the weekly rhythm with repeat participation and useful outcomes.

### Metrics
Qualified applications, accepted members, onboarding completion, weekly active members, attendance, problems resolved, useful introductions, month-two retention, willingness to pay and member referrals.

### Website promise
A private circle built around real context, direct feedback, useful introductions and execution. No fake member counts, testimonials or activity claims.

## 3. Performance

### Positioning
Practical consumer products that remove recurring friction from an active day. First validation track: Performance Meal Sets.

### Customer
People whose days move between work, training, travel and other commitments and who repeatedly spend time deciding, preparing or sourcing meals.

### V1 model
Brand/customer relationship owned by Performance; production handled by a validated food-production partner until demand justifies any deeper infrastructure.

### Validation path
Interest -> Interviews / waitlist -> Product specification -> Supplier quotes -> Packaging and food-safety requirements -> Delivery model -> Unit economics -> Small paid pilot -> Feedback -> Repeat purchase -> Repeatability -> Launch.

### Three gates before scale
1. Product: menu / portion structure, customer usefulness, repeat intent.
2. Operations: production partner, geography, packaging, shelf life, food safety, storage and delivery reliability.
3. Economics: full cost per set, delivery cost, waste, refunds/issues, contribution margin and repeat purchase.

### V1 readiness gate
A small paid pilot can be produced, delivered safely and repeatedly with measured economics and real repeat-purchase behaviour.

### Metrics
Waitlist, interview-to-buy intent, pilot conversion, cost per set, delivery success, issues/refunds, waste, contribution margin, repeat purchase and weekly retention.

### Website promise
Validation, not preorder. Do not present price, delivery area or launch date as final until confirmed.

## Shared operating model

Each owned venture must eventually have one canonical chain:
Buyer -> Problem -> Offer -> CTA -> Intake -> Qualification -> Payment (when applicable) -> Fulfilment -> QA -> Delivery -> Follow-up -> Proof -> Analytics.

Bootcamp / Content Creation + AI is not a fourth venture. It is the production and R&D layer feeding personal brand, Traffic Engine, VisibilityOS proof, Warriors acquisition and Performance lifestyle distribution.
